# Multi-Agent AI Research Assistant using Agentic RAG

## 📌 Project Overview

The Multi-Agent AI Research Assistant is an AI-powered system designed to automate and simplify the research paper analysis process using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and a Multi-Agent Architecture.

The system uses multiple specialized AI agents to search, retrieve, read, summarize, compare and analyze research papers. It aims to assist researchers and students in conducting literature reviews, understanding existing research and identifying potential research gaps.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Automate research paper retrieval and analysis.
- Generate concise summaries of research papers.
- Compare methodologies, techniques and experimental results.
- Identify research gaps and potential future directions.
- Generate structured literature reviews with citations.
- Coordinate multiple AI agents to perform complex research tasks.

## 🏗️ System Architecture

The system follows an Agentic RAG architecture consisting of:

1. **Research Agent:** Finds relevant research papers based on user queries.
2. **Reading Agent:** Extracts and summarizes important information from research papers.
3. **Comparison Agent:** Compares methodologies, results and approaches across papers.
4. **Citation Agent:** Extracts and manages research paper references.
5. **Report Agent:** Generates structured literature reviews and research reports.
6. **Coordinator Agent:** Manages agent communication and workflow execution.

## 🔄 Working Workflow

1. User submits a research query.
2. Coordinator Agent understands the query and manages the workflow.
3. Research Agent retrieves relevant research papers.
4. RAG pipeline processes and retrieves relevant paper content.
5. Reading Agent summarizes the selected papers.
6. Comparison Agent analyzes methodologies and findings.
7. Citation Agent manages references.
8. Report Agent generates the final research report.
